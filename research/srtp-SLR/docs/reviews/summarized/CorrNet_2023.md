# Continuous Sign Language Recognition with Correlation Network

## Basic Information
- Publisher/Date/Title: 2023, Continuous Sign Language Recognition with Correlation Network
- Authors & Affiliations: Lianyu Hu, Liqing Gao, Zekang Liu, Wei Feng (College of Intelligence and Computing, Tianjin University, China)

## Contributions & Features
1. Novel Correlation Network (CorrNet) for tracking body trajectories
2. Dynamic correlation map computation for spatial patches
3. Identification module for emphasizing body trajectories
4. Cross-frame trajectory analysis for sign identification

## Task
Improve continuous sign language recognition by explicitly capturing and leveraging body trajectories across video frames

## Challenges
1. Temporal Continuity:
   - Traditional methods process frames independently
   - Miss important trajectory information
2. Body Movement Tracking:
   - Need to track hands and face across frames
   - Complex spatial-temporal relationships
3. Feature Identification:
   - Distinguish relevant body movements
   - Dynamic emphasis of important trajectories

## Methods
1. Correlation Module:
   - Dynamic computation of correlation maps
   - Analysis of adjacent frames
   - Tracking spatial patches
2. Identification Module:
   - Dynamic emphasis of body trajectories
   - Trajectory-based feature extraction
3. Cross-frame Analysis:
   - Temporal relationship modeling
   - Continuous movement tracking
